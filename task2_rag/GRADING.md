# Grading

All answers were graded by hand by one person (the author), for the two conditions generated by `answer.py`: answers with the top 3 dense chunks ("retrieval") and answers with no retrieval.

## Rules

- **Figure questions (Q1 to Q3):** correct only if the answer gives the figure stated on the page snapshot.
- **List and comparison questions (Q4 to Q8):** correct if the answer covers at least 60% of the gold items in `questions.jsonl`.
- **Matching:** gold items are matched by equivalent meaning, not exact wording (for example "high sodium intake" counts for "unhealthy diets"). This matching rule was written after the answers were seen, so it is a post-hoc rule, not a pre-registered one. Where a grade depends on a borderline match, both readings are shown.
- **Partial:** the answer contains the required information but contradicts itself, abstains unnecessarily, or adds unsupported information next to an abstention.
- **Unanswerable questions (Q9 to Q12):** the right behaviour is to say the answer is not available. Answering from outside knowledge without saying so is "did not abstain".
- Extra, unsupported content in an otherwise correct answer is noted but does not change the grade.
